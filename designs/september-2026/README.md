# RCICS September 2026 Design

## Status

Active concept-development iteration.

> This design remains unvalidated and is not approved for clinical use.
> RCICS is intended for contact/droplet isolation support and is not an
> airborne-isolation or negative-pressure system.

## Objective

Develop a locally fabricable door assembly incorporating:

- A rigid viewing panel.
- Medical-line pass-through ports.
- A material-transfer chamber.
- Line strain relief.
- Sealable unused openings.
- Non-porous, disinfectant-compatible surfaces.
- Safe entry and emergency egress.

## Starting design direction

- Repurposed door as the assembly base.
- Plexiglas viewing panel.
- L-beam aluminum mounting frame.
- Gasketed panel-to-door interface.
- At least three medical-line pass-through ports.
- Separate material-transfer chamber.
- Replaceable or sealable port inserts.
- Local fabrication using commonly available tools.

## Provisional assumptions

- Three approximately 7 cm line ports.
- Silicone bungs or grommets, subject to availability.
- PVC-based port solution as fallback.
- Metal door preferred for the initial prototype.

## Open decisions

- Final number and diameter of ports.
- Clinical function assigned to each port.
- Plexiglas thickness.
- Transfer-chamber diameter and length.
- Gasket material.
- Fastener type, depth, and spacing.
- Mounting approach for metal and wooden doors.
- Line strain-relief mechanism.
- Prototype-build location.
- Validation procedure.
