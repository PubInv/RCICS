# RCICS August 2026 Concept Design

> Historical concept-design snapshot. This design has not been validated
> or approved for clinical deployment.

The complete August design snapshot is preserved in the Git tag:

`august-design-v0.1`

This iteration included:

- Repurposed door concept.
- Viewing-panel concept.
- Medical-line pass-through concept.
- PVC material-transfer chamber.
- L-shaped structural support.
- Initial FreeCAD models and technical drawings.
- Scaled components for 3D printing.

## Important limitations

Dimensions and material selections were provisional.

Known unresolved matters included:

- Transfer-chamber diameter.
- Port diameter and clinical allocation.
- Viewing-panel material and thickness.
- Fastener specification.
- Door-construction differences.
- Gasket and sealing details.
- Prototype validation.
